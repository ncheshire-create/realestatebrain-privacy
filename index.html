Real Estate Brain Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Real Estate Brain is published by Zbyt Studios. It is an unofficial study aid and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by any real estate commission, licensing board, or exam provider. Laws and exam content vary by state; always check your state's licensing requirements.

Data we collect

None. Real Estate Brain does not collect, store on our servers, share, or sell any personal information. The app has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics, and no third-party tracking.

Data on your device

Your quiz progress, scores, streak, and rank are stored only on your device so the app can show your progress. This data never leaves your device. Deleting the app or using Reset Progress in the app erases it.

Internet access

The app works fully offline and does not need an internet connection.

Children

The app collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page.
