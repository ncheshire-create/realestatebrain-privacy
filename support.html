Common questions

Where is my progress stored? Only on your device. Use Profile, then Reset progress, to clear it without deleting the app.

Does the app need internet? No, it works fully offline.

Is this my state's exam? The questions cover national principles. State laws and exam content vary, so check your state's licensing requirements.

Are these real exam questions? No. All questions are original and written for study. Real Estate Brain is an unofficial study aid and is not affiliated with any licensing board or exam provider.
