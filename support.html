Real Estate Brain Support

Real Estate Brain is published by Zbyt Studios. It is an unofficial study aid for the national portion of the real estate salesperson exam, and is not affiliated with any licensing board or exam provider.

Contact

Questions, bug reports, or suggested corrections: zbytstudios@gmail.com. We reply as soon as we can.

Common questions

Where is my progress stored? Only on your device. Deleting the app erases it. Use Profile, then Reset Progress, to clear it without deleting the app.

Does the app need internet? No, it works fully offline.

Is this my state's exam? The questions cover national principles. State laws and exam content vary, so check your state's licensing requirements. State-specific content may come later.

Are these real exam questions? No. All questions are original and written for study. Scores do not predict official results.
